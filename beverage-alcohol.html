The Challenge

You set the price. Two other tiers decide what the shopper pays.

In beverage alcohol, a price or promotion decision travels through a distributor and a retailer before it reaches the shelf or the back bar. Each tier takes its margin, and the rules change by state and province.

  • Trade investment grows, but it is hard to see how much of it reaches the shopper.
  • Shipments, depletions, and scan data tell three different stories.
  • Open, franchise, and control markets each need their own pricing logic.
  • Premiumization and down-trading happen at the same time, in the same portfolio.
  • On-premise and off-premise respond to very different levers.
